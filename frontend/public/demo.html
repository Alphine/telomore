Telomeres shorten every division — timing the response is the open question.

Maintenance and repair can slow it down, but both cost the cell something, and every real intervention condition costs a real culture run.

Telomere Evolution Lab searches that space in silico first, including organ-on-a-chip culture conditions — flow, oxygen, nutrients, stress — before anyone commits a physical chip.
